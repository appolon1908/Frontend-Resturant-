Questions before you ship?

Frequently asked questions

Exact pricing and timing depend on the shipment. An agent confirms the details with your quote.

How long does shipping take?

Transit time depends on U.S. origin, destination, sailing schedule, customs, and shipment type. The current estimate is provided with your quote.

How is the price calculated?

Pricing can depend on size, quantity, pickup location, destination, and handling needs.

Can you pick up at my home?

Pickup availability varies by city and shipment type. An agent can confirm pickup or the nearest drop-off option.

What items are restricted?

Restrictions vary by carrier and destination. Never pack hazardous, illegal, or undeclared items. Ask before packing anything uncertain.

Can I ship a vehicle?

Yes. Share the year, make, model, pickup city, and destination so an agent can explain requirements.
